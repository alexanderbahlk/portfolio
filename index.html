About me

"Co-founding WeSource in 2017 and building CoastSolutions solo in 2024 gave me a perspective most product leaders never get — I know what it costs to ship, and what it means to own the outcome."

Born and raised in North-East Germany, I started my career with an IT apprenticeship in 2001, then spent eight years in the German Navy before returning to technology in 2010.

After completing a Web Design and Development Bachelor's in Hamburg, I joined Goodgame Studios — helping scale the company from 15 to 1,000 employees and 200 million players before Berlin's startup scene pulled me in.

In 2017 I co-founded WeSource, an AI-ready recruitment platform that reached enterprise clients including Deutsche Bahn. My formal product leadership journey deepened at Product People, where I directed engagements for clients including WHO, Blinkist, and AVIV Group — and scaled the consultancy from 10 to 40 people.

In 2024 I founded CoastSolutions solo, shipping multiple B2C apps on Android and iOS. Today I'm also an Early-Stage Startup Advisor at Invest Ottawa, guiding 20+ founders on PMF and product strategy.
