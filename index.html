About me

"Founding a startup in 2017 gave me a perspective most product leaders never get — I know what it costs to ship, and what it means to own the outcome."

Born and raised in North-East Germany, I started my career with an IT apprenticeship in 2001, then spent eight years in the German Navy before returning to technology in 2010.

After completing a Web Design and Development Bachelor's in Hamburg — while interning at a game studio — I followed the pull of Berlin's startup scene, building product instincts across multiple companies and cultures.

In 2017 I co-founded my own startup, one of the best decisions of my career. My formal journey as a product leader began in 2020, deepened through years of client engagements with a leading product management consultancy.

Today I'm based between Barbados and Canada, bringing a rare combination of technical background, founder perspective, and product craft to every team I join.
